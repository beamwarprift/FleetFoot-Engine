# Changelog

Every release of FleetFoot Engine, newest first. Versions follow MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH. Each release in
the stores is built from the tag of the same version in this repository.

## 1.1.0

- Choose the language of the extension in the settings, or keep the browser's: the popup, the
  settings, the bar over the player and the right-click menu follow it.
- Show or hide the bar over the player with one click at the top of the popup, for every site.
- The preset speeds fold away under their title, and the popup remembers whether they are open.
- The titles of the settings sections stand out with a line under them.
- The favorite speed starts at 1.50× on a new install.
- The support link points to the developer's renamed page.

## 1.0.0

The first release.

- Speed control for any HTML5 video or audio, from 0.10× to 16×: presets, a favorite speed, the
  previous speed, and a speed you get while holding a key.
- Memory of your choice: nothing, one speed for every site, or a speed and a few settings per site.
- When a site keeps changing the speed back, you choose what happens: let it be, keep your speed
  with a brake, or keep it without one.
- Time left at the current speed, and the time playing faster has saved you.
- Skip Silence: the pauses play faster, at the speed you choose, and your speed comes back as soon
  as the sound does. The sound is heard only on your device, never recorded or sent.
- A–B loop, skips back and forward, picture-in-picture, natural voices at any speed.
- Several players on a page, players inside frames, pages that load new videos without reloading.
  When the player is in a frame from another site, the popup names that site and offers to allow it.
- Keyboard shortcuts on the page and in the browser, a bar over the player, a right-click menu.
- Access only where you allow it: when you call it, on the sites you choose, or on every site.
- Nothing sent anywhere: no telemetry, no accounts, no ads; settings only on your device, with
  export, import and delete-everything.
- Eighteen languages, from left to right and from right to left; ten color themes; the text of the
  settings page from 100% to 200%; works with a keyboard, a mouse or a finger.
